# 10 — Naming (undecided)

*Status: **open** — decide later. Domains are not bought yet.*

Requirements: works for both consumer and pilot products; not tied to glasses/HUD/Meta (we'll add phones, watches); easy to say to a voice assistant ("Hey Meta, open ___"); easy to spell.

| Brand | Consumer / Pilot | Domains (checked Oct 2026, not reserved) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **SkyCue** ⭐ | SkyCue / SkyCue Pilot | skycue.app, getskycue.com, skycueapp.com available; skycue.com taken | Safest all-rounder |
| **SkyTally** ⭐ | SkyTally / SkyTally Pilot | skytally.app, skytallyapp.com available; skytally.com taken | "Tally" = pilot slang for spotting traffic + counting spotted planes. "Tally" = TallyPrime in India |
| Overhear | Overhear / Overhear Pilot | overhearsky.com available; overhearapp.com registered Sep 2026 | Confusable with competitor "Overhead" |
| PlaneWhisper | PlaneWhisper / PlaneWhisper Pilot | planewhisper.com unregistered | Playful; less serious for pilots |
| SkyCallout | SkyCallout / SkyCallout Pilot | skycallout.com unregistered | Literal, a bit long |
| HeadsUp Pilot | pilot only | headsuppilot.com unregistered | Original working name; doesn't fit consumer app |

Rejected: SkyHUD (taken, existing software, "HUD" unclear to consumers), SkyIntel (taken, existing tracking project, sounds like surveillance), Skyglazz (sounds like competitor app SkyGlass, bad for voice, hard to spell).

Before deciding: search Play Store, App Store, USPTO trademarks; say it aloud to a voice assistant.
