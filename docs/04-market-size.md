# 04 — Market Size: How Many Hobby Pilots

*Researched: 3 October 2026.*

## United States (official FAA data, end of 2025)

| Category | Active certificates |
| --- | --- |
| **Private pilots** (main hobby group) | **174,155** |
| **Sport pilots** (light aircraft, mostly for fun) | **7,450** |
| Student pilots (future private pilots, possible early users) | 370,286 |
| Commercial pilots | 118,314 |
| Airline transport pilots | 181,742 |
| **Total active pilots** | **887,519** |

- Hobby-pilot pool: ~**180,000**.
- Not all fly purely for fun, and some rarely fly → realistic core of **~100,000–150,000 actively flying hobby pilots**.

## Worldwide (rough estimate)

- No international body keeps an exact count; rules differ by country.
- The US is often described as having close to half of all pilots.
- UK: ~43,000 private pilots (2008 data, old). Canada, Europe, Australia add more.
- Private pilots outside the US: perhaps **150,000–250,000** → **~350,000–450,000 worldwide**.

## What this means

- Meta glasses are sold mainly in the US, Canada, UK, Europe, Australia — the same places most hobby pilots are. Good overlap.
- Real customers = pilots who own **both** Meta glasses **and** an ADS-B receiver → a small slice.
- **Illustration only:** 2% of US private pilots × $100/year ≈ 3,500 subscribers ≈ **$350,000/year**. Solid niche, not huge → global expansion and student pilots matter later.
