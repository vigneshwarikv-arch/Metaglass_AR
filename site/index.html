How it works

No wiring into the aircraft. Your phone does the work and the glasses become its screen and speaker.

Built as a co-pilot, not an instrument

  • Supplementary only. HeadsUp Pilot adds situational awareness. It never replaces your certified instruments or your scan.
  • VFR first. Designed for daytime visual flying by private and sport pilots.
  • Glanceable by design. A few numbers and one alert at a time. Audio carries the rest.
  • You're the pilot in command. Turn it off with one tap or a voice command.
