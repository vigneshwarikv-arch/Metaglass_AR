Questions

Which glasses work with it?

Meta Ray-Ban Display (spoken answer plus a small card in the lens) and Ray-Ban Meta (spoken answer). Glasses are optional — everything also works on the phone.

Which phones are supported?

Android first. iPhone is planned next.

Where does the flight data come from?

From live flight-tracking networks that receive the position signals aircraft broadcast. Some planes don't share their route, so a few details may show as unknown.

Does it work everywhere?

It works wherever flight-tracking networks have coverage, which includes most busy airspace. You need an internet connection.

How much does it cost?

The core features will be free. Early-access testers help us decide what comes next.
